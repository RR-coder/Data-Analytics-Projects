# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Prediction

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns and identifies key factors influencing employee turnover. The objective is to help HR teams understand workforce risks, improve employee retention, and support data-driven decision making.

The project combines:

- Python for data cleaning, EDA, and machine learning
- SQL for business analysis
- Tableau for dashboard development
- Predictive analytics for attrition risk assessment

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition increases:

- Recruitment costs
- Training costs
- Productivity loss
- Knowledge loss
- Workforce instability

The goal is to identify high-risk employee groups and recommend strategies to improve retention.

---

## Dataset Information

Dataset: IBM HR Employee Attrition Dataset

Records:

- 1,470 Employees

Features:

- Age
- Department
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction
- Overtime
- Years At Company
- Work-Life Balance
- Attrition Status
- and other workforce attributes

## Key Business Insights

### Attrition Rate

Overall employee attrition rate:

**16.12%**

Approximately 1 out of every 6 employees leaves the organization.

---

### Overtime Impact

Employees working overtime showed nearly **3x higher attrition rates** compared to employees without overtime.

---

### Age Risk

Employees under 30 years old experienced the highest attrition rates.

---

### Tenure Risk

Employees with 0–2 years tenure were significantly more likely to leave.

---

### Job Satisfaction

Lower job satisfaction levels were strongly associated with higher attrition.

---

## Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

Reduce excessive overtime through better workforce planning.

### Recommendation 2

Implement retention programs for employees within their first two years.

### Recommendation 3

Improve employee engagement and job satisfaction.

### Recommendation 4

Monitor high-risk departments and job roles.

### Recommendation 5

Use predictive analytics to proactively identify employees at risk of leaving.
