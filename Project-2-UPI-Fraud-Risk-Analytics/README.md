# UPI Fraud & Risk Analytics

## Project Overview

Designed and developed an end-to-end fraud analytics solution to identify suspicious UPI transactions, high-risk merchants, fraud hotspots, and payment channel vulnerabilities.

The project simulates how fintech companies and digital payment platforms monitor fraud patterns and support risk investigation teams.

---

## Business Problem

Digital payment ecosystems process millions of transactions daily.

Fraud analysts must quickly identify:

- Fraudulent users
- High-risk merchants
- Fraud hotspot cities
- Risky payment methods
- Transaction failure trends

This project builds an analytical command center to support fraud detection and investigation.

## Key Insights

- Delhi and Mumbai generated the highest fraud volumes.
- UPI and Wallet transactions showed elevated fraud activity.
- Certain merchants accumulated significantly higher risk scores.
- Fraud activity was concentrated among a small subset of users.
