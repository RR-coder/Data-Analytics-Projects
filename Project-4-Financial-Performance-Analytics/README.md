# Financial Performance & Profitability Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, profitability, customer segments, products, regions, and geographic performance using Python, SQL, and Tableau.

The goal is to identify revenue drivers, profit-generating categories, loss-making products, regional performance trends, and business growth opportunities.

---

## Business Problem

Management wants to understand:

- Which regions generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which products contribute most to profitability?
- Which products are causing losses?
- Which customer segments are most valuable?
- How has business performance changed over time?
- Which states are driving growth and profitability?

## Key Insights

### Regional Performance

- West region generated the highest revenue and profit.
- Central region showed the lowest profitability.
- Profit margins varied significantly across regions.

### Category Analysis

- Technology generated the highest profit.
- Office Supplies delivered strong profitability.
- Furniture generated high sales but relatively low profit.

### Customer Segment Analysis

- Consumer segment contributed the highest revenue and profit.
- Corporate segment ranked second.
- Home Office segment generated the lowest revenue.

### Product Analysis

Top profitable product:

- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier

Major loss-making products:

- Cubify CubeX 3D Printer
- Chromcraft Conference Tables
- Bush Conference Tables

### Geographic Analysis

Most profitable states:

- California
- New York
- Washington

Some high-revenue states generated lower profitability, highlighting margin optimization opportunities.

### Growth Analysis

Revenue increased consistently from 2014 to 2017, indicating strong business growth.
